An eye for an eye only ends up making the whole world blind

Walter

沃爾特 范 貝倫多克

Discover now

A story about Walter Van Beirendonck’s disgust towards terror and violence.

To address what is going on in the world, Walter Van Beirendonck has released an unofficial trilogy of collections, all aiming to bring attention to one major problem.

Pr test

抗議

In 2006 the first collection dropped. Even it’s title made the message very clear.

To further his protest, Walter turned this entire collection into a wearable protest banner. On the pieces he added bold slogans directly addressing the terror and violence.

He makes use of a striking quote to paint violence as a failure of empathy instead of a power.

The same quote later gets used at a more politically charged time to portray a very important message.

Anger & Rage

憤怒與暴怒

A decade later, in 2016, Walter’s album called “WOEST” is a representation of his frustration turning into anger and even rage.

This happened because his warnings ended up going unheard for too long. He could not stand by as the world got destroyed by terror, violence and intolerance.

The violence and aggression Walter once addressed now crawls into his designs.

The elegance and grace of his pieces are disrupted by aggressive elements like masks, spikes, and harsh graphics.

The coexistence of rage and beauty mirrors a seemingly civilised society that grows more and more intolerant and cruel.

Healing

療癒

While the tensions deepens in the final collection, Walter does not succumb to the darkness he expressed in the previous collection.

The “ZWART" collection is a turning point where he turns his darkness into a space for transformation.

Making use of pagan rituals, animal symbols, and mythical creatures, fashion gets the role of a healing force.

Several pieces in this collection have giant gloves. These are not meant as weapons, but rather as a gesture of care and protection.

Epilogue

外帶

The trilogy demonstrates how violence systematically destroys empathy and creativity, leaving a void that only collective resistance can fill. Imagination and human connection are the ultimate tools to overcome this issue.
