An eye for an eye only ends up making the whole world blind

Walter

沃爾特 范 貝倫多克

A story about Walter Van Beirendonck’s disgust towards terror and violence.

To address what is going on in the world, Walter Van Beirendonck has released an unofficial trilogy of collections, all aiming to bring attention to one major problem.

Pr test

抗議

In 2006 the first collection dropped. Even it’s title made the message very clear.

To further his protest, Walter turned this entire collection into a wearable protest banner. On the pieces he added bold slogans directly addressing the terror and violence.

He makes use of a striking quote to paint violence as a failure of empathy instead of a power.

The same quote later gets used at a more politically charged time to portray a very important message.
